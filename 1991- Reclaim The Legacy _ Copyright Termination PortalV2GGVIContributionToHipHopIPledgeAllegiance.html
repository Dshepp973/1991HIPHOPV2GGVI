DECODING THE CRATES

PUBLISHING VS. MASTERS

Publishing refers to the underlying composition (lyrics & melody). This is almost always reclaimable by the songwriter. Masters are the actual audio recordings. Labels often fight harder for these, claiming they were "Works Made For Hire." However, many 1991 contracts fail the strict legal test for "Work For Hire," giving artists a fighting chance to reclaim masters too.

THE "SAMPLE" TRAP

Hip Hop is unique because it relies on samples. When you terminate rights to a song like "O.P.P." (which samples Jackson 5), you only reclaim ownership of your new contributions (lyrics, flow, new drums). You do not reclaim the rights to the sample used; that license usually remains with the label or must be renegotiated.
